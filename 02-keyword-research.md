## 2. Intent Dissection: The "Trap" of Volume vs. Revenue

### The Consumer Trap: `ai video generator`
- **Why it looks attractive:** Massive search volume (100k+ monthly queries).
- **The harsh reality:** 95% of searchers are searching for "free Sora clone", "Runway gen 3 free tier", or "anime video generator without watermark".
- **Conversion rate to a paid $500–$2,000/mo B2B service:** Under 0.2%. Ranking for this query generates high bounce rates and support tickets asking for free browser software.

### The Profit Champion: `AI Video Production Services`
- **Why it wins:** While raw volume is lower (~8k - 15k monthly targeted queries), **every single searcher has an approved corporate budget**.
- **Search Intent:** "I don't want to learn prompt engineering or spend 40 hours inside After Effects. I need someone to take my product and hand me finished, high-converting video ads."
- **Customer Lifetime Value (LTV):** $3,000 – $24,000 annually.

### The Ad Spend Champion: `AI UGC Video for Brands`
- **Why it wins:** DTC and e-commerce founders are actively fatigued by hiring TikTok creators, shipping physical samples, and waiting 3 weeks for low-effort iPhone selfie videos.
- **Search Intent:** "Can I generate high-converting UGC video ads for my product using AI without waiting weeks for influencers?"
- **Cost-Per-Acquisition Advantage:** Matches White Mask's "First 3 Videos Free — Submit Product URL" offering with zero friction.
